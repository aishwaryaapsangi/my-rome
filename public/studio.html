🧳 How this app gets its numbers

✅ Direct airline sources

Delta official booking and ITA official route booking. These are the authoritative checkout links.

They don't expose 31 days × four-seat × 10-day public prices in a form we can responsibly scrape and attribute.

📊 Published 31-day benchmarks

SlickTrip 14-day monitored airfare calendar, one adult, any airline and up to one connection. This fills the exploratory heatmap immediately.

Not equivalent to a 10-day flight, and not live Delta or ITA inventory.

🔌 Optional real API

Duffel Flight Offers API can be connected to the serverless endpoint after deployment. Quotes are from a flight supplier rather than Delta/ITA websites.

The API token must stay on your server; Duffel test mode produces non-realistic data.

📅 What does ten days mean?

Departure and return dates inclusive: e.g. July 19–28. Fly overnight, arrive July 20, then spend eight nights in Italy, fly back July 28.

Selected dates and itineraries are proposals until tickets and reservations are bought.
